# Content Accuracy (~400 tokens)

The page teaches beginners, so facts must be right. Check every changed fact against a primary source.

## Preferred Sources
- **Government of India, Ministry of Tourism / Incredible India** — official destination information: https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in
- **UNESCO World Heritage List** — inscription status and dates: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/
- **Government of India, Know India portal** — states and Union Territories: https://knowindia.india.gov.in

Use Wikipedia only to find primary sources, not as the citation itself.

## Known Issues on the Current Page
| Item | Current text | Issue |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Leh-Ladakh | "Jammu and Kashmir" | Ladakh has been a separate Union Territory since 31 Oct 2019 (J&K Reorganisation Act, 2019). Should read "Ladakh (Union Territory)". |
| Goa | "Western India" | Vague. Goa is a state; "Goa (state), west coast" is clearer. |
| Andaman Islands | "Bay of Bengal" | Accurate geographically; the administrative unit is the Union Territory of Andaman and Nicobar Islands. |

Fix these only when the user asks; log the fix in a completion doc.

## Enrichment Ideas (verify before use)
UNESCO status would add credibility: the Taj Mahal (1983), Group of Monuments at Hampi (1986), Jaipur City (2019), and the Darjeeling Himalayan Railway (1999, part of "Mountain Railways of India") are all inscribed sites. Confirm each on the UNESCO list before adding.

## Style
- Active voice, short sentences, no superlatives you cannot source ("most famous" → cite or soften).
- Use "State" or "Union Territory" explicitly in location lines.
